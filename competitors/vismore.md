# Vismore

## Overview
**Website:** https://www.vismore.ai  
**Category:** AI Search Visibility / Execution-Layer AEO Platform  
**Founded:** ~2025  
**HQ:** Unknown (EU/international)  
**Status:** Early-stage; self-funded or angel-backed (no public funding disclosed)

Vismore is an execution-layer AEO platform that explicitly positions itself against "monitoring-only" tools. Its differentiator: rather than just showing visibility metrics, it closes the loop between insight → content strategy → publishing. The platform enables one-click publishing to Reddit, Medium, and LinkedIn — which is genuinely unique among AEO tools. Vismore is actively cited in Reddit's r/DigitalMarketing and r/webmarketing AEO comparison threads as one of the top "action-oriented" tools.

## Key Features
- **Prompt monitoring** — Tracks brand visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity
- **Prompt prioritization** — Tells you which prompts/opportunities to pursue first by impact
- **Content strategy → publishing workflow** — Turns visibility gaps into specific content recommendations, then pushes content to Reddit, Medium, and LinkedIn directly (unique capability in category)
- **Publication credits** — Priced per content piece published, not just per prompt monitored
- **Competitor visibility comparisons** at the prompt level
- **AI Visibility Maturity Model** (proprietary framework) — positions the brand in maturity stages for marketing messaging
- 7-day free trial, no credit card required

## Strengths
1. **Distribution-as-strategy is genuinely unique:** No other AEO tool at this price tier publishes content directly to Reddit, Medium, and LinkedIn. This closes the insight → action loop that most tools leave open.
2. **Execution framing resonates:** Reddit users specifically call Vismore out as "feels more like a growth tool than a tracking tool." This is the market's #1 complaint about monitoring dashboards.
3. **Transparent pricing** with a real free trial — low friction entry
4. **Content marketing positioning is smart** — they're actively engineering AI citations by publishing comparison content that names themselves alongside Profound, Peec, Otterly

## Weaknesses
1. **Very limited platform coverage:** ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity only at launch — no Claude, AI Mode, Copilot, DeepSeek, Grok
2. **Publishing credits are a new metric that buyers must learn to value** — unclear ROI on publishing to Reddit/Medium vs. first-party content
3. **No crawler logs, GSC integration, or Reddit citation tracking** at the monitoring layer — these are Promptwatch differentiators
4. **Small team** = product risk if growth stalls or founders leave
5. **No established brand recognition** — cited in conversations but not yet in major "best of" roundups
6. **No disclosed funding** means limited ability to compete on data infrastructure with Profound ($155M)

## Competitive Threat to Promptwatch
**MODERATE and GROWING.** Vismore directly competes for the "I need to take action, not just monitor" buyer — which is the dominant pain point in AEO in 2026. Their $99/month entry tier directly overlaps with Promptwatch's Essential plan. The one-click publishing to Reddit/Medium/LinkedIn is a genuine differentiator that Promptwatch does not match (Promptwatch tracks Reddit citations but doesn't publish to Reddit). **Threat to watch:** If Vismore expands platform coverage and develops a stronger content marketing presence, it could become a meaningful alternative for mid-market buyers who want execution workflows over deep monitoring.
