# Orchly.ai

## Overview
**Website:** https://orchly.ai  
**Category:** All-in-One AEO + SEO Platform (Content + AI Visibility)  
**Founded:** ~2025  
**HQ:** India (founder Shivam Kumar, based in India)  
**Status:** Early-stage; self-funded (bootstrapped)

Orchly.ai combines traditional SEO content creation with AEO/GEO visibility tracking in a single platform. It is explicitly positioned as a low-cost alternative to tools like Promptwatch, Profound, and Scrunch AI — with a $49/month entry price that is significantly lower than most dedicated AEO tools. The founder (Shivam Kumar) is active on LinkedIn and has published comparison content specifically targeting Promptwatch buyers.

## Key Features
- **AI Search Monitoring** — Tracks brand visibility across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews
- **SEO + GEO combined** — Traditional keyword rank tracking + AI visibility in one dashboard
- **Content creation agents** — AI article writing, optimization, and content refresh (25–50 articles/month depending on plan)
- **Action Center** — Prioritized list of specific content tasks: what to write, what to update, which external sites to pitch for citations
- **Website SEO & GEO Audit** — On-page optimization for both traditional and AI search
- **AI Traffic Analytics** — Bot traffic + human traffic analytics (who's visiting, what AI crawlers are doing)
- **WhiteLabel Reports** — Available on Pro plan ($125/month) for agency use
- **Agency Pitches** — Create branded reports for potential clients (5 per month on Pro)
- **ChatGPT Shopping Visibility** — Tracks product visibility in ChatGPT shopping
- **Query Fan-Out Analysis** — Shows how AI expands prompts into sub-queries (feature overlap with Promptwatch)
- **Auto CMS Publishing** — Content publishes directly to WordPress, Webflow, etc.
- **Google Search Console integration**
- 14-day free trial

## Target Customer
Individual marketing specialists, growing brands, and agencies who want both SEO and AEO tracking without paying for two separate tools. Budget-conscious teams who can't justify $99–$249/month for monitoring-only. Particularly relevant for content-first teams who want to automate content publishing alongside visibility tracking.

## Strengths
1. **Lowest price in the AEO + SEO combined category** at $49/month with daily prompt refresh — undercuts Promptwatch's $99 Essential by 50%
2. **Broadest platform coverage from entry tier** — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews (7 platforms at $49/month)
3. **Combined SEO + AEO** — If a team is starting fresh, Orchly avoids the need for two separate subscriptions
4. **Agency features at low price** — Whitelabel reports and agency pitches at $125/month vs. Promptwatch's custom tier for white-label
5. **Auto CMS publishing** — Content creation → publish to WordPress/Webflow automatically (fewer tools in the stack)
6. **Query fan-out analysis** — Feature parity with Promptwatch on this specific report

## Weaknesses
1. **No crawler log analysis** — Promptwatch's unique technical capability for understanding which pages AI crawlers read
2. **No Reddit citation tracking** — Promptwatch-specific differentiator
3. **No YouTube citation tracking** — Promptwatch-specific differentiator
4. **No MCP integration** — Promptwatch has programmatic developer access
5. **Limited brand recognition** — Not cited in major third-party AEO roundups yet; primarily self-cited
6. **Bootstrapped with very small team** — Data infrastructure at scale is a real risk
7. **SEO/content-first positioning** may mean AI monitoring is the secondary feature, not primary
8. **Unproven at scale** — 14,300 content pieces generated (self-reported) is not a large dataset

## Competitive Threat to Promptwatch
**MODERATE.** At $49/month with 7+ platform coverage and combined SEO+AEO, Orchly is a direct price pressure on Promptwatch's Essential tier ($99/month, 1 website, 50 prompts). Budget-conscious teams and solo marketers who are evaluating Promptwatch may choose Orchly simply on price. The agency pitch reports at $125/month are also lower than Promptwatch's custom agency tier. **However:** Orchly lacks Promptwatch's three most unique differentiators (crawler logs, Reddit tracking, MCP integration), which are the features that retain sophisticated buyers. The real threat is at the entry and mid-market tier where price sensitivity is highest.
