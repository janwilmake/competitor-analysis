# SE Ranking — SE Visible (AI Search Toolkit)

## Overview
**Website:** https://seranking.com  
**Category:** SEO Platform with AI Visibility Add-on  
**Founded:** 2013  
**HQ:** London, UK  
**Status:** Private, bootstrapped/VC-backed (funding details limited)

SE Ranking is an all-in-one SEO platform with an optional **AI Search Toolkit** add-on (branded as "SE Visible") that provides AI answer engine tracking. Positioned as the value leader in the combined SEO + AEO category — lower price point than Ahrefs or Semrush equivalents.

## Key Features (AI/AEO-specific)
- Tracks AI Results across four AI search engines with daily monitoring for up to 1,000 prompts (tier-dependent)
- **Unlimited AI competitive research** across tracked platforms — competitor brand mentions, citations, and sentiment without domain limits
- **SE Visible Dashboard:** Strategic AI visibility data integrated into the SE Ranking platform
- **Value pricing:** Often cited as the most affordable option among SEO-suite AEO tools

## Pricing
- SE Ranking base: From $71/month (Essential) to $119/month (Business)
- AI Toolkit add-on: Additional cost (exact figure not confirmed; total reported at $71–119/month range including add-on)
- Enterprise: Custom

## Target Customer
SEO teams and agencies that want a more affordable alternative to Ahrefs/Semrush for combined SEO + AI visibility tracking.

## Recent News (as of April 2026)
- Consistently featured in "Profound alternatives" and "Peec AI alternatives" roundups as the all-in-one value choice
- Gaining traction among agencies managing multiple client accounts at accessible price points

## Strengths
- Best price-to-feature ratio for combined SEO + AEO
- Unlimited competitive research across AI platforms
- Familiar SEO-native workflow for existing users
- Good for agencies managing multiple clients at scale

## Weaknesses
- Covers only 4 AI engines vs. 6 for Otterly or more for Profound/Promptwatch
- Less specialized depth in AI-specific features vs. pure-play AEO tools
- Less brand recognition than Semrush or Ahrefs in buyer evaluation cycles

## Competitive Threat to Promptwatch
**MODERATE** for budget-conscious buyers already in the SE Ranking ecosystem. Less of a threat for buyers specifically evaluating Promptwatch's source-level citation and crawler analytics features.
