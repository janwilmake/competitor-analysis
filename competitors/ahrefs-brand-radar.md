# Ahrefs — Brand Radar (AI Visibility Add-on)

## Overview
**Website:** https://ahrefs.com  
**Category:** SEO Platform with AI Visibility Add-on  
**Founded:** 2010  
**HQ:** Singapore  
**Status:** Private, profitable, ~$100M+ ARR

Ahrefs is one of the world's leading SEO platforms. In 2025–2026, it launched **Brand Radar**, an add-on providing AI search performance tracking powered by one of the largest AI query databases in the market (193M+ organic prompts sourced from actual search queries). Competes directly in AEO/GEO tracking for existing Ahrefs customers.

## Key Features (AI/AEO-specific)
- **193M+ organic prompts** sourced from actual search data (not synthetic) across 6 AI platforms
- **AI Share of Voice:** Brand mention percentage vs. competitors with multi-brand comparison and position tracking within AI-generated answers
- **Topic clustering:** Groups AI prompts by theme to identify content gaps where competitors appear but you don't
- **6 AI platforms tracked** including YouTube, TikTok, and Reddit visibility (broader than pure-play AEO tools)
- **Full SEO suite integration:** AI visibility alongside traditional rank tracking, backlinks, site audit — one platform for SEO + AEO

## Pricing
- Ahrefs base plans: From $199/month
- AI add-on (Brand Radar): Reportedly $199+/month as an add-on
- Enterprise: Custom

## Target Customer
Enterprise organizations, SEO teams already invested in Ahrefs, digital marketing agencies managing large brand portfolios.

## Funding & Team
- Private, estimated 900+ employees
- Bootstrapped and profitable; no VC funding
- Highly regarded as a best-in-class data company

## Recent News (as of April 2026)
- Brand Radar add-on gaining traction as an add-on for existing Ahrefs customers evaluating AEO tools
- Named as a best-for-enterprise alternative in multiple "Profound alternatives" lists (Q1 2026)
- Continues expanding AI-sourced query database; 193M+ prompts from real search data is a major data advantage

## Strengths
- **Massive data advantage:** 193M+ real search queries vs. synthetic prompt sets competitors use
- **Broadest source coverage:** Includes Reddit, TikTok, YouTube in addition to AI engines
- **Existing customer base:** Millions of SEO teams already in Ahrefs will naturally try the add-on
- **Combined SEO + AEO:** One platform removes tool proliferation
- **Trust and brand credibility:** 15+ years as a top-tier SEO tool

## Weaknesses
- AI features are secondary to core SEO — less depth and innovation speed than pure-play AEO tools
- Expensive when bundled with required Ahrefs base subscription
- Best for existing customers; hard to justify for teams not already using Ahrefs
- Less actionable AI-specific recommendations vs. purpose-built GEO tools

## Competitive Threat to Promptwatch
**HIGH** for mid-to-large SEO teams already on Ahrefs. Many buyers evaluating Promptwatch will already be Ahrefs customers and may prefer to use the add-on rather than adopting a new vendor. This is a serious horizontal platform threat.
