# Bluefish AI

## Overview
**Website:** https://www.bluefishai.com  
**Category:** Enterprise AI Visibility / GEO Platform — Citation Analytics & Brand Safety  
**Founded:** 2024  
**HQ:** USA  
**Status:** VC-backed (seed funding from Bloomberg Beta; ~$24M total funding per industry sources)

Bluefish AI occupies a distinct niche in the GEO/AEO market: it is an **analytics-first enterprise platform** focused on *understanding where AI citations come from* (source influence, citation patterns, semantic drivers) rather than simple brand mention tracking. It differs from most tools by treating the question as "how does AI search actually work for my brand?" rather than "do I appear in AI search?" Bluefish's enterprise positioning targets Fortune 500 brands that need brand safety, metadata governance, and deep citation intelligence. Featured in Adweek for original citation research. Appears in Scrunch's "7 best AEO/GEO tools for 2026" list.

## Key Features
- **Multi-engine AI visibility monitoring:** Real-time tracking across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google Summary, and additional platforms
- **Advanced citation analytics:** Source-level breakdowns showing exactly where AI platforms pull citations from — which sites, which content types, which social platforms
- **Model-aware diagnostics:** Reveals how different AI models interpret your brand differently; source influence, citation patterns, and semantic drivers at the model level
- **AI Brand Vault:** Metadata governance system — controls how AI models interpret and represent brand data; claims 97% cross-engine brand consistency rate
- **Impact & Influence Analytics:** Measures which external sources are actually shaping AI recommendations about your brand
- **Favorability, sentiment, safety, accuracy scoring:** More granular brand representation metrics than simple mention tracking
- **AI Brand Safety:** Monitors for AI-generated misrepresentation, hallucination, and reputation risks
- **Agentic Commerce:** Features for when AI agents (not humans) are the primary product discovery interface
- **Enterprise workflows:** SOC 2 compliance (in progress), SSO, RBAC, permissioned data governance
- **Professional services/consulting:** Expert-led GEO advisory, model-reasoning reviews, enterprise change management
- **Audience segmentation:** Tracks brand visibility differences by audience demographic (age, budget, etc.)

## Strengths

## Competitive Threat to Promptwatch

If Promptwatch moves upstream into enterprise deals, Bluefish will compete on analytical depth and brand safety.
