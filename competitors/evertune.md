# Evertune

## Overview
**Website:** https://www.evertune.ai  
**Category:** AI Search Visibility / GEO Platform (Enterprise)  
**Founded:** April 2024  
**HQ:** New York City, USA  
**Status:** VC-backed, Series A — led by ex-The Trade Desk executives

Evertune is an enterprise-grade GEO/AEO platform built by veterans from The Trade Desk. It positions itself as the most analytically rigorous option in the market, with a focus on **source influence analytics** (which sources actually drive AI citations), **unlimited competitor tracking**, and **statistical rigor** at scale via base model API access. In their own "Top 15 GEO Platforms for 2026" roundup, they rank themselves #1 (obvious bias — but demonstrates category sophistication).

## Key Features
- **Source Influence Analytics:** Identifies which source pages and domains actually cause AI platforms to cite or mention your brand — a layer deeper than simple mention tracking
- **Unlimited Competitor Tracking:** Track any number of competitor brands across all monitored prompts
- **Base Model API Access:** Connects directly to LLM APIs for statistical significance in prompt sampling (not just web interface scraping) — 1M+ prompts per brand per month
- **Foundational Model Intelligence:** Tracks how AI models' base knowledge (training data) differs from consumer app responses
- **AI Brand Index:** Benchmarking database across categories
- **Prompting at Scale:** High prompt volumes with statistical rigor
- **AI Retargeting:** Proprietary retargeting capabilities for brands visible in AI answers
- **Platform coverage:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Meta AI, DeepSeek

## Strengths
- Source influence analytics is genuinely differentiated — no other tool at this depth identifies *why* citations happen
- Base model API access provides more accurate/reliable data than browser-scraping approaches
- 1M+ prompts/brand/month is the largest prompt volume in the category
- Leadership team credibility: The Trade Desk background brings serious operational maturity
- AI Retargeting is a future-looking capability no one else has publicly announced
- Strong analytical depth for teams that need research-grade data

## Weaknesses
- $3,000/month entry price puts it out of reach for all but large enterprises
- Relatively new — still less proven track record than Profound in enterprise procurement
- Unknown brand recognition outside early adopter enterprise circles
- Sales-only motion may slow adoption rate
- Self-ranking reports hurt credibility; difficult to trust their own assessments

## Competitive Threat to Promptwatch
**MODERATE-HIGH** at the enterprise tier. Evertune's source influence analytics and 1M+ prompt volume is a meaningfully superior feature for high-sophistication buyers. At $3,000/month, Evertune is not competing for Promptwatch's core mid-market buyers — but if Promptwatch pursues enterprise contracts, Evertune will win on analytical depth. Worth monitoring as the enterprise AEO benchmark alongside Profound.
