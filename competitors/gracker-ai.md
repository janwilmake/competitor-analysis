# GrackerAI

## Overview
**Website:** https://gracker.ai  
**Category:** AEO / GEO Platform — B2B SaaS & Cybersecurity Specialist  
**Founded:** ~2024  
**HQ:** San Francisco, CA, USA (One Market St, 36th floor)  
**Status:** Early-stage; funding status undisclosed

GrackerAI is a full-stack GEO platform specifically designed for B2B SaaS companies, with a particular emphasis on cybersecurity, DevTools, fintech, and identity/access management (IAM) verticals. Unlike horizontal AEO tools, GrackerAI combines AI visibility monitoring with an automated content generation engine specifically tuned for technical B2B audiences. It explicitly competes with Promptwatch (has a "GrackerAI vs PromptWatch" comparison page) and positions its vertical specialization as the key differentiator.

## Key Features
- **AI Visibility Monitoring** — Tracks brand mentions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and Google AI Overviews (6 engines monitored daily)
- **Competitive Citation Analysis** — Shows exactly which queries trigger competitor citations and where you're absent
- **AEO & GEO Scoring** — Weekly visibility scores tracking improvement over time
- **Programmatic GEO** — Automated content creation at scale targeting AEO gaps
- **Editorial GEO** — High-quality human-assisted content for complex technical topics
- **GEO Avalanche / GEO Heist** — Proprietary content strategies (details not publicly disclosed)
- **GEO Agents** — AI agents for content creation and distribution automation
- **GEO Copilot** — Content assistant for creating citation-optimized content
- **Industry Benchmark Reports** — Publishes industry-specific AI visibility benchmarks (e.g., "State of AI Search Visibility in Cybersecurity 2026")
- **Keyword & Prompt Research** — Discovers relevant prompts buyers use in AI search
- **Trend Research & Industry Insights** — Category-level AI visibility data

## Strengths
1. **Vertical specialization is a genuine differentiator** — No other AEO tool has gone deep on cybersecurity/B2B SaaS. In complex technical markets, vertical expertise is a real buying signal.
2. **Industry benchmark data** — Publishing "State of AI Visibility in Cybersecurity" creates category authority and lead generation simultaneously.
3. **6-engine monitoring from entry tier** (including Copilot, which many tools don't cover) — matches Promptwatch's engine breadth at the same $99 price point
4. **Full-stack option** — For companies that want monitoring + content creation + strategy, the enterprise tier at $3,500/month bundles everything
5. **Named Promptwatch as a competitor** — Direct comparison pages generate organic traffic from Promptwatch searchers

## Weaknesses
1. **Niche focus limits TAM** — B2B SaaS/cybersecurity is a large but specific market; horizontal AEO tools serve every industry
2. **Very high enterprise price** ($3,500/month+) limits accessibility to well-funded companies
3. **No crawler log analysis** — Promptwatch's unique technical capability
4. **No Reddit or YouTube citation tracking** — Promptwatch-specific differentiators
5. **Limited public customer evidence** — 500+ users claimed but no named logos from major cybersecurity vendors publicly confirmed
6. **Funding opacity** — Unclear whether the company has institutional backing or capital to scale data infrastructure
7. **Limited third-party reviews** — Very few independent G2/Capterra reviews (2 on Software Advice, pricing "upon request")

## Competitive Threat to Promptwatch
**LOW-MODERATE overall; MODERATE-HIGH for B2B SaaS segment specifically.** GrackerAI's vertical focus means it primarily steals deals from Promptwatch in the B2B tech/cybersecurity segment, not broadly. However, because it has a "GrackerAI vs PromptWatch" comparison page, it is actively intercepting Promptwatch evaluation traffic. Teams at cybersecurity or B2B SaaS companies may choose GrackerAI for its industry expertise over Promptwatch's broader horizontal approach. Promptwatch should consider whether a B2B SaaS/vertical landing page could recapture this segment.
