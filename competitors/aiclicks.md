# AIclicks

## Overview
**Website:** https://aiclicks.io  
**Category:** AI Search Visibility / AEO Monitoring & Optimization  
**Founded:** 2024  
**HQ:** Lithuania (founders based in Vilnius; EU-based)  
**Status:** Early-stage, bootstrapped-lean, 2–9 employees; growing agency base

AIclicks is a GEO/AEO platform that positions itself around the combination of monitoring, source intelligence, and actionable content recommendations in one workflow. Its founders are from the SEO/growth community (Lithuanian-based). The platform claims ~1,000+ businesses as customers, has an active G2 and Trustpilot presence, and is increasingly appearing in "best AEO tools" roundups. Key differentiator is the **Sources report** (showing which domains are cited by LLMs for monitored queries) combined with weekly AI recommendations and AI-assisted content creation — all at an approachable price point. 4.9/5 on G2 (small review base).

## Key Features
- **LLM monitoring** across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI, and Copilot (10+ engines)
- **Competitor benchmarking** — share of voice vs. competitor set per prompt
- **Source intelligence** — which website domains AI platforms cite for monitored queries (similar to Promptwatch's citation source tracking)
- **Weekly AI recommendations** — prioritized action list for improving visibility
- **AI-assisted content creation** — generates content pieces targeting citation gaps (10–20/month depending on plan)
- **Query fan-out analysis** — maps how AI expands prompts into sub-queries
- **Country-level tracking** (unlimited on Pro+)
- **Google Analytics integration** (Pro+)
- **Agency program** — multi-client management
- **3-day free trial** (no credit card)
- **Actionable audit** — flags issues like missing FAQs, schema gaps, robots.txt blocks

## Strengths
- Sources report (domain-level citation intelligence) is valuable and similar to Promptwatch's citation source tracking
- Low entry price ($59/month) with 10+ LLM coverage — competitive with or cheaper than Peec AI
- Weekly actionable recommendations (not just dashboards) addresses execution gap
- Content generation included in all plans (10–20 pieces/month)
- 3-day free trial with no credit card lowers evaluation friction
- 4.9/5 G2 score (small base, but consistently positive early reviews)

## Weaknesses
- Very small team (2–9 employees) — scalability and support capacity are real limitations
- G2 and Trustpilot review bases are small (8–9 reviews each) — limited independent validation
- No crawler log analysis (Promptwatch differentiator)
- Weekly rather than daily refresh on Starter
- Limited enterprise security features (no SOC 2, SSO mentioned)
- Unknown brand recognition in non-European markets

## Competitive Threat to Promptwatch
**LOW-MODERATE.** AIclicks competes at the entry tier (under $100/month) with a similar feature surface to Promptwatch's Essential plan. The source intelligence feature is directly analogous to Promptwatch's citation tracking. For price-sensitive buyers, especially in Europe, AIclicks presents a credible lower-cost alternative. However, the very small team, limited validation, and lack of crawler logs limits competitive pressure for Promptwatch's mid-market and above buyers. Worth monitoring for growth trajectory — if they hit 10,000+ customers and raise funding, they could move up-market.
