# LLM Pulse

## Overview
**Website:** https://llmpulse.ai  
**Category:** AI Search Visibility / AEO Monitoring  
**Founded:** ~2024  
**HQ:** Europe (likely Spain/EU, based on EUR pricing and customer testimonials)  
**Status:** Bootstrapped, profitable — self-described "bootstrapped since day one"

LLM Pulse is a self-serve AI search visibility platform focused on transparent pricing, a genuine free trial, and broad AI engine coverage from the entry tier. It is bootstrapped and explicitly profitable, which the team uses as a marketing signal to differentiate from VC-funded competitors. Broad feature surface including citations, sentiment, share of voice, prompt discovery, content recommendations, white-label, API, MCP integration, and Looker Studio. Appears frequently in "Promptwatch alternatives" and "most affordable AEO tool" comparisons.

## Key Features
- **Prompt tracking** across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews, Gemini (self-serve); additional models on Enterprise
- **Citation analysis** — which sources are cited alongside brand mentions
- **Sentiment analysis** — how AI describes your brand
- **Share of voice** vs. competitors
- **AI prompt suggestions & prompt research** — discovers prompts where your brand could appear
- **Query fan-out analysis** — maps how AI expands a prompt into sub-queries
- **Content intelligence & recommendations** — suggests content based on visibility gaps
- **Web analytics integration** (GA4 and similar)
- **Looker Studio connector**
- **White-label capability** — specifically marketed to agencies
- **API & MCP integration** — MCP server allows tools like Cursor and Claude Desktop to query LLM Pulse data
- **App tracking** (mobile app visibility in AI results)
- **Tags & project organization** — flexible segmentation
- **14-day free trial** (no credit card required)

## Strengths
- Lowest cost per tracked prompt among European platforms with comparable coverage
- Real 14-day free trial (no credit card) — significantly lowers evaluation friction
- Bootstrapped/profitable model creates product discipline; no growth-at-all-costs pressure
- White-label capability is valuable for agencies; one of the clearest agency-focused offerings
- Full feature stack (citations, sentiment, SoV, prompt research, content recs, MCP, Looker Studio) across all plans
- MCP integration and API depth rivals larger funded tools
- Transparent pricing helps procurement compared to demo-only competitors

## Weaknesses
- Weekly tracking cadence (vs. daily on many competitors) — data latency is a drawback for fast-moving brands
- Limited brand recognition compared to Profound, Scrunch, or Promptwatch in most "best tools" roundups
- EU-only pricing (EUR) adds friction for US buyers
- Bootstrapped model limits speed of headcount/infrastructure scaling if demand accelerates
- Limited public case studies or enterprise validation
- No crawler log analysis (a Promptwatch differentiator)

## Competitive Threat to Promptwatch
**MODERATE-HIGH** in the agency and budget-conscious mid-market segment. LLM Pulse directly competes for Promptwatch's most price-sensitive buyers: agencies managing multiple clients, and teams in Europe who want to evaluate before committing. The white-label capability specifically competes with Promptwatch's agency tier positioning. The cost-per-prompt advantage (LLM Pulse €0.99/prompt vs. Promptwatch ~$1.98/prompt at Growth tier) is a real and marketable difference. If LLM Pulse improves tracking cadence to daily and builds more public case studies, they become a serious threat at the core of Promptwatch's customer base.
