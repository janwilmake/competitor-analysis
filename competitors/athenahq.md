# AthenaHQ

## Overview
**Website:** https://athenahq.ai  
**Category:** AI Search Visibility / GEO Platform  
**Founded:** ~2024 (YCombinator company)  
**HQ:** San Francisco, CA, USA  
**Status:** Seed-funded, active — founded by Google Search + DeepMind veterans

AthenaHQ is a GEO/AEO platform purpose-built for AI search optimization, founded by engineers with direct experience at Google Search and DeepMind. Unlike tools that started as SEO platforms and added AI features, AthenaHQ was built from the ground up for generative engine optimization. Its enterprise differentiator is the **Athena Citation Engine (ACE)** — a proprietary system for improving citation probability. Listed in Evertune's "Top 15 GEO Platforms 2026" at position #2 (with $2.7M seed funding noted).

## Key Features
- **8 LLM Coverage:** ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Grok (+ additional models on request)
- **AI Blindspot Detection:** Identifies where AI has gaps or incorrect knowledge about your brand
- **Competitor Monitoring & Impersonation Tracking:** Unlimited competitor tracking; monitors if AI is misattributing competitor claims to your brand
- **Granular Authority & Citation Intelligence:** Detailed analysis of which sources drive AI citations
- **Dynamic AI Crawling:** Finds hidden or lost pages on your website that AI crawlers aren't indexing
- **Action Center:** Surfaces specific opportunities ranked by impact (vs. generic dashboards)
- **Prompt Volume Estimation (Enterprise):** Estimates actual search demand for prompts in AI engines — proprietary model, not traditional SEO keyword volume
- **Athena Citation Engine (ACE) (Enterprise):** Proprietary system for improving citation probability
- **Content Optimization AI Agent (Enterprise):** Self-improving content creation workflows
- **LLM Traffic Analysis (Enterprise):** Connect AI visibility to revenue via Shopify + GA4 integrations
- **SAML/OIDC SSO, Unlimited seats with RBAC** on all plans

## Strengths
- Google Search + DeepMind founder background provides genuine technical credibility
- $95/month entry makes it one of the most affordable tools covering 8 LLMs
- Action Center genuinely differentiates from pure dashboards — surfaces actionable priorities
- Unlimited seats on all plans removes per-seat pricing friction
- LLM traffic to revenue attribution (Shopify + GA4) answers the CFO question most tools can't
- AI Blindspot Detection and Impersonation Tracking are unique features
- Dynamic AI crawling for finding lost/hidden pages not indexed by AI bots

## Weaknesses
- $2.7M seed funding is thin compared to Profound ($155M) or Brandlight ($30M)
- Prompt Volume estimation and advanced ACE are locked to enterprise — key features unavailable to self-serve customers
- Relatively low brand recognition vs. Profound, Evertune, and Scrunch
- Limited public customer case studies vs. competitors
- Being YCombinator-backed helps but doesn't guarantee scaling resources

## Competitive Threat to Promptwatch
**MODERATE-HIGH** at the mid-market tier. At $95/month with 8 LLMs covered and unlimited seats, AthenaHQ is directly price-competitive with Promptwatch's Essential plan. The Action Center approach (priority-ranked opportunities) and unlimited seats may be more compelling than Promptwatch's dashboard-first interface for teams at the same price point. The revenue attribution via Shopify/GA4 is a differentiator Promptwatch lacks. Watch this one — well-funded competitors with founder credibility at this price point are the sharpest challenge for Promptwatch's core market.
