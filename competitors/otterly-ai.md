# Otterly AI

## Overview
**Website:** https://www.otterly.ai  
**Category:** AI Search Visibility / GEO Audit + Monitoring  
**Founded:** 2023  
**HQ:** Persenbeug, Lower Austria, Austria (HQ); Santa Clara, CA (US presence)  
**Status:** Funded startup, 11–50 employees; growing rapidly

Otterly AI (OtterlyAI GmbH) positions itself as an affordable AEO/GEO platform combining automated monitoring across six AI engines with a GEO audit tool that diagnoses why a site isn't earning AI citations. The platform has experienced significant traction in 2025–2026: **30,000+ users** as of June 2026 (up from 20,000 in February 2026), named to **G2's 2026 Best Software Awards (Top 10 Rookies of the Year)** and the **only AEO platform in the Top 10**, and won the **European Search Awards 2026 — Best AI Search Software Solution** (May 20, 2026, Porto). The AI Search category on G2 grew +2,000% in one year. Founded by Thomas Peham (former VP Marketing at Storyblok), Josef Trauner, and Klaus M. Schremser (co-founders of Usersnap, sold to saas.group). Bootstrapped — no external VC funding. Covered by TechCrunch (December 2024). Recognized as a Gartner Cool Vendor in AI in Marketing (2025).

## Key Features
- **GEO Audit Tool:** Evaluates 25+ on-page factors and provides actionable recommendations for why content isn't being cited
- **AI Keyword Research:** Discovers conversational queries audiences use in AI search — surfaces prompt opportunities before competitors
- **Share of AI Voice / Brand Visibility Index:** Benchmarks brand citation percentage vs. competitors across all tracked engines; automatically tracks visibility over time
- **Website Citation Report:** Enhanced citation insights and exports for operationalizing citation data
- **Recommendations feature (new, 2026):** Translates monitoring insights into prioritized optimization actions — moves platform beyond pure monitoring
- **YouTube citation tracking:** OtterlyAI published the first large-scale YouTube citation study (100M citations analyzed) — YouTube is the #2 social platform for AI citations
- **6 AI engines tracked:** ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot
- **Noble partnership:** Connected citation intelligence with targeted digital outreach execution (Feb 2026)
- **Research publishing:** Published major citation datasets (1M+ AI citations, 100M citation YouTube study) that generate significant press coverage

## Strengths
- GEO audit feature is a useful differentiator — tells users *why* they aren't getting cited, not just *that* they aren't
- **20,000+ users** is the largest reported user base of any pure-play AEO tool — demonstrates strong product-market fit at the affordable tier
- G2 Rookies of the Year recognition — only AEO tool to crack the Top 10, significant category validation
- Research publishing strategy (YouTube citations study, 95% third-party source study) generates organic press and establishes data authority rivaling Promptwatch's 1B+ citation dataset
- Noble partnership bridges citation intelligence to execution — addresses the "insight gap" directly
- Bootstrapped model; no investor pressure to change pricing or direction
- Broadest engine coverage (6 platforms) at its price point

## Weaknesses
- Base plan limits prompt count to 15 — very restrictive for real use
- Less raw data depth than Promptwatch (no crawler logs, no GSC integration)
- Small team (11–50), limited resources for rapid feature development
- Public API is new (June 2026) — ecosystem of integrations not yet proven at scale
- No Looker Studio connector (Peec AI has one, which agencies prefer)

## Competitive Threat to Promptwatch
**MODERATE-HIGH (upgraded from MODERATE).** Otterly now has 30,000+ users, a European Search Awards win, and a full developer API + Claude Skill that rivals Promptwatch's MCP integration. The community workflow marketplace (101+ workflows at launch) is a clever low-cost ecosystem play — Promptwatch has no equivalent. Their research publishing strategy generates significant organic authority. The European Search Awards win provides media credibility that many competitors lack. Otterly won't win large enterprise deals, but they are clearly the default starting point for teams entering the AEO space, and their API launch means they can now compete for developers and agencies who want programmatic access — Promptwatch's previous differentiation on MCP integration is now partially matched.
