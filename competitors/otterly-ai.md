# Otterly AI

## Overview
**Website:** https://www.otterly.ai  
**Category:** AI Search Visibility / GEO Audit + Monitoring  
**Founded:** ~2024–2025  
**HQ:** Unknown (appears to be European/international)  
**Status:** Early-stage, active

Otterly AI positions itself as an affordable entry-level AEO/GEO platform combining automated monitoring across six AI engines with a GEO audit tool that diagnoses why a site isn't earning AI citations.

## Key Features
- **GEO Audit Tool:** Evaluates 25+ on-page factors and provides actionable recommendations for why content isn't being cited
- **AI Keyword Research:** Discovers conversational queries audiences use in AI search — surfaces prompt opportunities before competitors
- **Share of AI Voice:** Benchmarks brand citation percentage vs. competitors across all tracked engines
- **6 AI engines tracked** (broadest coverage by engine count in this tier)
- **Affordable pricing** for solo marketers and small agencies

## Pricing
- Base plan: limited prompt counts (15 prompts)
- Lacks Google Looker Studio Connector on base plan
- Exact pricing not confirmed; positioned as budget-friendly alternative to Profound ($99+)

## Target Customer
Solo marketers, small business owners, agencies managing SME clients. Buyers who need affordable entry-level monitoring with optimization guidance.

## Funding & Team
- Very early stage; no public funding information
- Small team

## Integrations
- 6 AI engines (exact list not fully confirmed; includes major platforms)

## Recent News (as of April 2026)
- Featured in "Profound alternatives" and "Peec AI alternatives" roundups in Q1 2026
- No major product or funding news

## Strengths
- GEO audit feature is a useful differentiator — tells users *why* they aren't getting cited, not just *that* they aren't
- Broadest engine coverage (6 platforms) at its price point
- Good for content teams wanting optimization guidance not just monitoring

## Weaknesses
- Base plan limits prompt count to 15 — very restrictive for real use
- Less data depth than Profound or Promptwatch
- Small team, limited resources for rapid feature development
- Missing some integration features (Looker Studio) competitors have

## Competitive Threat to Promptwatch
**LOW.** Otterly targets the budget SMB segment. Promptwatch's Answer Gap analysis and source-level citation tracking are more sophisticated. Otterly may win at the very low end of the market but is unlikely to compete for Promptwatch's core buyers.
