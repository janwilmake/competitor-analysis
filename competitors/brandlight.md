# Brandlight

## Overview
**Website:** https://www.brandlight.ai  
**Category:** Enterprise AI Visibility Platform / AI Marketing OS  
**Founded:** October 2024  
**HQ:** Tel Aviv, Israel (offices in USA)  
**Status:** Series A-funded, enterprise-focused

Brandlight is an Israeli enterprise AI visibility startup that positions itself as the "control room" for the rise of the **agentic web** — where AI search, commerce, ads, and AI agents converge into a unified marketing channel. Rather than just tracking AI citations, Brandlight aims to be a full AI marketing operating system, with modules for partnership, content, SEO, technical analysis, social, and more. Featured in Adweek, Wired, Ad Age, and The Drum. Named itself the "#1 AEO platform globally" in its own marketing (treat with appropriate skepticism).

## Key Features
- **AI Visibility Monitoring:** Real-time tracking of brand mentions across AI platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and 11+ platforms total)
- **Sentiment & Narrative Analysis:** How AI describes your brand and product positioning
- **Competitive Intelligence:** How competitors appear vs. your brand in AI responses
- **Partnership Module:** Identifies partnership opportunities based on AI citation patterns
- **Content Module:** Content strategy aligned with AI citation signals
- **Technical SEO Module:** AI readiness audits for technical optimization
- **Social Module:** AI visibility across social-influenced AI answers
- **AI Advertising Intelligence:** Preparing brands for AI-native advertising as platforms like OpenAI introduce ad products
- **Agentic Web Navigation:** Tools for when AI agents (not humans) are the primary discovery interface
- Processes **billions of AI-generated signals** daily
- Integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce ecosystem (claimed enterprise integrations)

## Pricing
- **Enterprise-only pricing** — not publicly listed

## Strengths
- **Largest Series A in pure-play enterprise AEO** outside of Profound ($30M vs. Profound's $96M Series C)
- **Fortune 500 validation** — LG, Kimberly-Clark, Estée Lauder are credible logos that help enterprise procurement
- **Agentic web positioning** is forward-looking — bets on AI agents (not just AI search) as the next major distribution channel
- **AI advertising intelligence** is a unique future capability no other tool is currently building
- Adweek pitch deck publication suggests strong PR/communications ability
- "Central cockpit" concept — comprehensive AI marketing OS vs. point solutions

## Weaknesses
- **No self-serve option** — enterprise-only sales motion limits total addressable market
- **Very new company** (October 2024) with limited track record despite strong funding
- Pricing ($4,000–$15,000/month) limits market to only large enterprises
- 35-person team is stretched thin to cover all the modules they claim
- "Central cockpit" / "AI marketing OS" framing may be overly broad for buyers who want focused solutions
- Named themselves "#1 globally" — self-assertion without third-party validation

## Competitive Threat to Promptwatch
**LOW for current market; HIGH for future positioning.** At $4,000–$15,000/month, Brandlight does not compete for Promptwatch's core buyers. However, Brandlight's "AI marketing OS" positioning and $30M funding represent a long-term threat if the AEO market consolidates around a few well-funded platforms. More immediately, Brandlight sets the expectation at the enterprise tier that monitoring must connect to advertising, partnerships, and agentic distribution — capabilities Promptwatch doesn't yet have. If Promptwatch's agency and enterprise customers start comparing against Brandlight, the gap becomes a sales objection.
