### Key Findings This Run

#### Market Clarification
- **CRITICAL:** Promptwatch.com is an **AI Search Visibility / AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) / GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** platform — it helps brands track and improve their visibility inside AI-generated search responses (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity). It is NOT an LLM developer observability tool (not in the LangSmith/Langfuse category).
- Promptwatch was founded in 2025 by Gijs de Groot and Klaas Foppen (Amsterdam). Raised $1.2M seed from Arches Capital in September 2025.
- Promptwatch pricing: Essential ~$89/month, Growth $199/month, Agency/Enterprise custom.
- Promptwatch's crawler log analysis feature (showing which pages AI crawlers index and cite) appears to be **uniquely differentiated** — no direct competitor offers this at the same level.

#### Market Trends
- AEO/GEO category growing rapidly; Gartner predicts 60% enterprise adoption of AI observability platforms by 2028 (from 18% today)
- 37% of product discovery queries now start in AI interfaces (ChatGPT, Perplexity)
- 40-60% of AI-cited sources change month-to-month — making continuous monitoring essential
- Biggest market gap: bridging monitoring insight to content execution
- Traditional SEO platforms (Ahrefs, Semrush, SE Ranking) aggressively adding AI visibility features — major horizontal platform threat to pure-play AEO startups
