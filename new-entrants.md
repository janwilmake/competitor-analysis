## Notes on Market Dynamics (April 2026)
- **G2's AI Search category grew +2,000% in one year** — the market is real and accelerating
- **94% of CMOs plan to increase AEO/GEO investment in 2026** (Conductor CMO survey) — budget is following attention
- **31.3% of the US population will use generative AI search in 2026** (EMARKETER forecast)
- **Monitoring-only tools are being commoditized** — the competitive edge is now "insight → action" (content generation, recommendations, optimization workflows)
- **AXP / parallel machine-readable web** (Scrunch) represents a fundamentally different approach — not just measuring AI visibility but actively shaping what AI reads
- **Traditional SEO platforms (Semrush, Ahrefs, Conductor, BrightEdge)** are entering the category with distribution advantages that pure-play tools can't match on channel alone
