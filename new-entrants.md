## Notes on Market Dynamics
- The AEO/GEO tool category barely existed 2 years ago. Gartner projects 60% of engineering teams will adopt AI observability platforms by 2028 (up from 18% today), and a parallel market is forming on the *marketing* side for AI search visibility.
- Between 40–60% of cited sources change month-to-month across major AI platforms, making visibility highly volatile and continuous monitoring essential.
- The biggest unmet need in the market is bridging *insight* to *execution* — most tools show dashboards but don't help teams do anything about their visibility gap.
- Traditional SEO giants (Semrush, Ahrefs, SE Ranking) are aggressively adding AI visibility features, creating a horizontal platform threat.
