## What Is Promptwatch.io?

**[Promptwatch.io](https://www.promptwatch.io)** is an **AI Search Visibility platform** (also called AEO — Answer Engine Optimization, or GEO — Generative Engine Optimization). It helps marketing teams track and improve their brand's presence inside AI-generated responses from ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and related AI search engines.

**Not** an LLM developer tool or model observability platform — it is squarely a *marketing/SEO tool* for brands wanting to appear in AI answers.

### Key Product Capabilities (as of April 2026)
- Prompt-level brand visibility tracking across 9+ AI platforms (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Meta/Llama, DeepSeek, Grok)
- AI crawler log analysis — which pages AI bots are reading and citing (claimed differentiator)
- Offsite citation tracking — which third-party sources drive AI recommendations of your brand
- Answer Gap analysis — prompts where competitors appear but you don't
- Content generation agents — articles optimized for AI citation
- Google Search Console + Looker Studio integration
- Agency/white-label tier (custom pricing)
- Visitor analytics with AI traffic attribution
- Entity tracker, sentiment analysis, competitor benchmarking

## Summary of Key Findings (April 2026)

### 🔴 Critical Threats

**1. Profound — Now a Unicorn ($1B, $155M raised)**
Profound raised $96M Series C led by Lightspeed (with Sequoia, Kleiner Perkins) in February 2026. They repriced downward — $99/mo entry is now directly competing with Promptwatch's Essential tier. They launched **Profound Agents** (autonomous content generation integrated with monitoring). Serving 700+ enterprise brands including Target, Walmart, Figma, and 10% of Fortune 500. This is the dominant force in the category.

**2. HubSpot AEO Grader — Free, Unlimited, Massive Distribution**
HubSpot launched a completely free AEO grader (no credit card, unlimited use) covering ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini with 5-dimension brand scoring. With HubSpot's 200,000+ customer base, this directly attacks the top of Promptwatch's funnel. Teams evaluating basic AI visibility monitoring may start here and never pay for a tool.

**3. Horizontal Platform Threat (Ahrefs, Semrush, SE Ranking)**
These platforms have large installed SEO customer bases. Adding AI visibility as an add-on or bundled feature is low-friction for their existing customers.

### 🟠 Significant New Entrants

**Brandlight** — Israeli startup, raised $30M Series A (Feb 2026, Pelion Venture Partners). Serves Kimberly-Clark, LG, Estée Lauder, The Hartford. Positioning as an enterprise "AI marketing OS." Founded Oct 2024, 35 employees. Premium pricing ($4,000–$15,000/month reported).

**AthenaHQ** — Founded by Google Search + DeepMind veterans. Raised $2.7M seed (June 2025). $95/month entry price with 8 LLMs tracked. G2 recognition. Athena Citation Engine (ACE) as enterprise differentiator.

### 🟡 Existing Competitor Updates

- **Evertune**: Confirmed $19M Series A (Felicis Ventures, Aug 2025). Entry pricing confirmed at $3,000/month. 40+ employees.
- **Scrunch AI**: Confirmed $26M total funding ($15M Series A July 2025 led by Decibel). Launched AXP (Agent Experience Platform) — "parallel machine-readable website" for AI crawlers.
- **Peec AI**: Entry price updated to €89/month (Starter, 25 prompts). Pro at €199/month.

### 📊 Market Context (April 2026)

- **AI search market share** (April 2026): ChatGPT 60.2%, Gemini 15.3%, Microsoft Copilot 12.8%, Perplexity 5.5%, Claude 4.9%, Grok 0.6% (US market had Grok at 17.8% in Jan 2026 by one study — figures vary by methodology)
- **Claude** is the fastest-growing chatbot (+14%/quarter) — important for citation monitoring focus
- **Google AI Mode** is now a separate tracking channel from AI Overviews, adding complexity
- **Monitoring-only tools are being commoditized** — of 73 platforms launched in the past year, fewer than 25% raised meaningful funding. Investors are backing optimization platforms (Profound $96M, Brandlight $30M, Scrunch $15M, Evertune $19M).
- The "parallel web" concept (Scrunch's AXP, Brandlight's approach) is emerging as the next frontier — not just tracking citations but actively shaping what AI reads.

### 🟢 Where Promptwatch Stands

Promptwatch is a well-positioned mid-market tool with genuine technical differentiators (crawler log analysis, GSC integration, offsite citation tracking). Growing user base (8,150+ customers), positive G2 reputation (4.7/5), and expanding coverage (now tracks DeepSeek, Meta/Llama, Grok). 

However, the funding gap is severe: Promptwatch has raised $1.4M vs. Profound's $155M. The product roadmap execution pressure is intensifying. The biggest risk is that Profound's downward pricing ($99/mo entry) now directly overlaps Promptwatch's core pricing tier, with significantly more resources and brand recognition behind it.

Promptwatch's path: either grow into a clear mid-market leader on depth of data (crawler logs, citation chains) and agency/multi-client support, or get squeezed between free tools (HubSpot) at the bottom and well-funded platforms (Profound, Brandlight) at the top.
